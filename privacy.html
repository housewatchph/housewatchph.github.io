Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy covers HouseWatch Pipeline, the private tool that HouseWatch PH ("we") uses to prepare and upload its own videos, and this website. HouseWatch Pipeline is used only by the owner of the HouseWatch PH channel and pages. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information from viewers.

What the tool accesses

When the owner signs in, HouseWatch Pipeline asks for permission to act on the owner's own accounts, and only for these purposes:

  • YouTube (Google): upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, add them to the channel's playlists, and read the channel's own video list and basic details, all on the HouseWatch PH channel.
  • Facebook (Meta): see which Pages the owner manages and publish videos and Reels to the HouseWatch PH Page.
  • TikTok: read the account's basic profile and publish or send videos as drafts to the HouseWatch PH account.

The tool does not read, collect or store viewers' personal information, comments, messages, contacts or analytics about other people.

What is stored, and where

  • The sign-in keys (access tokens) that these services issue are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder. They are never sent to anyone else and are not stored on a server.
  • The videos, titles and descriptions the tool creates are stored on the owner's computer and uploaded only to the owner's own accounts.
  • We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any data obtained from Google, Meta or TikTok to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google API Services

HouseWatch Pipeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Google/YouTube: remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Facebook: remove access in Facebook Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  • TikTok: remove access in TikTok Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services.

Removing access stops the tool immediately. Because the tool is used only by the channel's owner, the stored sign-in keys are deleted from the owner's computer whenever access is removed. For any privacy question, send us a message on our YouTube channel or the HouseWatch PH Facebook Page.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers and does not collect any personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

HouseWatch Pipeline is not directed to children and is used only by the channel's adult owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Send us a message on our YouTube channel or the HouseWatch PH Facebook Page.
